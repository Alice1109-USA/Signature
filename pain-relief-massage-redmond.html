Targeted Massage for Areas of Tension

Pain relief massage is a service focused on areas of muscle tension and discomfort. It is intended as personalized bodywork for comfort, not diagnosis, a cure or a replacement for care from a healthcare professional.

How Your Session Is Customized

Tell your therapist where you feel tension, what pressure feels comfortable and which areas you want to avoid. The session can prioritize your stated goals without treating deeper pressure as the only way to personalize a massage.

Common Focus Areas

Guests may request attention to back, neck or shoulder tension and other tight-feeling areas. If pain is new, severe or related to an injury or medical condition, seek appropriate healthcare advice before arranging massage.

Pain Relief vs. Deep Tissue Massage

Pain relief describes the goal of the appointment; deep tissue describes a firmer, focused approach. A pain-relief session does not automatically mean maximum pressure. Compare the deep tissue service and discuss your needs before choosing.

Add-Ons & Session Options

FAQs

Is pain relief massage the same as deep tissue?

No. Pain relief describes an appointment goal, while deep tissue describes a focused, firmer technique.

Can I request focus on my back or neck?

Yes. Explain which areas you want to prioritize before the session.

Can pressure be adjusted?

Yes. Your comfort and feedback should guide pressure preferences.

What if I have an injury or medical condition?

Consult your healthcare provider when appropriate and share relevant restrictions before booking. Massage does not diagnose or cure an injury.
