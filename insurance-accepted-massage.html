Insurance Accepted

We accept coverage for muscle pain, joint discomfort, chronic tension, and functional limitations — making therapeutic massage more accessible for our patients in Redmond.

Home › Insurance Accepted

How It Works

1

Email Your Insurance Card

Send a photo or copy of your insurance card before your first appointment so we can verify your coverage ahead of time.

2

We Verify Your Coverage

For in-network plans, we handle verification and billing directly on your behalf — you focus on your treatment, we handle the paperwork.

3

Superbill If Out-of-Network

If you're out-of-network, we provide a Superbill so you can file a claim directly with your insurer for reimbursement.

4

Enjoy Your Session

Receive the same personalized, licensed care as any client — your insurance handles the payment, you enjoy the results.

A Few Honest Notes on Coverage

  • We are currently in the process of credentialing with additional insurance providers — call us to check where things stand with your specific plan.
  • Even with in-network status, individual plans may have restrictions such as visit limits, prior authorization requirements, or referral needs.
  • While we attempt to verify your benefits beforehand, your insurance company makes the final payment determination.
  • Out-of-network patients receive a Superbill to self-submit for reimbursement.
