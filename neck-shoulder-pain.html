When Desk Work Leaves Your Shoulders Feeling Tight

Long periods at a computer can leave the upper back, base of the neck and tops of the shoulders feeling tense. A neck and shoulder massage in Redmond can give these areas focused time without turning the visit into an aggressive full-body session.

Your therapist may work around the upper trapezius, shoulder blades and nearby upper-back muscles based on what you report. Pressure can be changed at any point, and you can ask to avoid a sensitive area.

What to Expect During Your Session

Before the massage, identify where the tension feels most noticeable and whether it changes with sitting, reaching or turning your head. The appointment can prioritize the neck, shoulders and upper back while still allowing time for surrounding areas when appropriate.

New, severe or unexplained pain should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional. Massage is offered for comfort and relaxation and is not a diagnosis or guaranteed pain treatment.

Neck & Shoulder Massage FAQs

Can I book a massage mainly for tight shoulders?

Yes. Tell the therapist that your shoulders and upper back are the priority so the available session time can be planned around those areas.

Is this the same as deep tissue massage?

Not necessarily. This page describes the focus area; pressure may be gentle, moderate or firmer depending on your preference and what is appropriate.
