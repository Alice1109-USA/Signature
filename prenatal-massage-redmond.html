Massage Designed for Pregnancy Comfort

Prenatal massage is a pregnancy-focused appointment planned around comfort and individual needs. Discuss your stage of pregnancy and any guidance from your maternity care team before booking at Signature Wellness SPA in Redmond.

Supportive Positioning & Personalized Pressure

Positioning and pressure should be discussed before treatment. Tell us which positions are comfortable and ask what support is available for your visit. You can request adjustments or stop the session if you feel uncomfortable.

What to Expect

Share relevant health information, pressure preferences and areas you would like to avoid. A prenatal visit is not simply a standard appointment with a different name: the planning conversation should reflect your pregnancy and current comfort.

When to Check With Your Healthcare Provider

Ask your obstetrician or midwife whether massage is appropriate, especially for a high-risk pregnancy, complications or restrictions. We do not state a universal safe starting week or provide medical clearance through this website.

Prenatal Massage Pricing

A 60-minute prenatal massage is $110. Contact us before booking if you have questions about positioning or suitability.

View current pricing and session options.

Prenatal FAQs

How are prenatal massage sessions positioned?

Contact us to discuss comfortable positioning and the support available for your appointment.

Can pressure be adjusted?

Discuss pressure before treatment and request changes whenever necessary.

When should I ask my healthcare provider first?

Ask first if your pregnancy is high-risk, you have complications or your care team has given restrictions.

What should I tell my therapist before the session?

Share your stage of pregnancy, relevant care guidance, positioning needs and any areas you want avoided.
