What Is a Deep Cleansing Facial?

A deep cleansing facial focuses on cleansing and the appearance of surface buildup or congestion. It is cosmetic skin care, not treatment for acne or another medical skin condition.

Cleansing, Exfoliation & Pore-Focused Care

Discuss cleansing, suitable exfoliation and pore-focused steps with our team. The final approach depends on the selected service and your skin; ask whether extractions are appropriate and included instead of assuming every visit contains them.

Who May Enjoy This Facial?

This may appeal to guests concerned about oily areas, visible buildup or congested-looking pores. Share sensitivities, recent procedures and current products so you can discuss the most suitable appointment.

What to Expect

Begin with a conversation about your skin goals and comfort. Confirm the treatment steps, products and any aftercare guidance before the facial. This service does not imply use of a branded HydraFacial device.

Pricing

FAQs

Is this facial good for oily or congested-looking skin?

It focuses on the appearance of buildup and congestion, but suitability should be discussed for your skin.

Does it include exfoliation?

Ask which exfoliation steps are suitable and included in your selected appointment.

How is it different from a hydrating facial?

Deep cleansing focuses on buildup and pores; hydrating care emphasizes moisture and comfort.

What should I do before my appointment?

Share sensitivities, products and recent skin procedures, and ask the team for preparation guidance.
