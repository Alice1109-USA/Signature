What Is Deep Tissue Massage?

Deep tissue massage is focused bodywork using firmer pressure where appropriate. At Signature Wellness SPA, the conversation starts with your areas of muscle tightness and your pressure preferences rather than a promise that stronger is always better.

Firm Pressure for Tight Muscles & Knots

A session can concentrate on tight-feeling muscles or areas commonly described as muscle knots. Firm pressure is adjusted to your comfort, and a longer appointment may allow time for focused work within a broader full-body massage. Massage does not diagnose the source of pain or guarantee relief from an injury.

What to Expect

Discuss your goals and health considerations before treatment. Pressure should remain tolerable, and you can ask for adjustments throughout the appointment. Tell your therapist about sharp pain or discomfort instead of trying to push through it.

Deep Tissue vs. Swedish Massage

Deep tissue is more targeted than a typical Swedish session, while Swedish emphasizes flowing strokes and a gentler relaxation experience. Neither choice needs to hurt. Your preferences help determine which approach is a better fit.

Session Lengths & Pricing

Deep tissue massage is $60 for 30 minutes, $80 for 60 minutes, $120 for 90 minutes and $160 for 120 minutes. Ask which session length fits the number of areas you want to discuss.

Deep Tissue FAQs

Does deep tissue massage have to hurt?

No. Pain is not a requirement for an effective or appropriate session. Ask for an adjustment if pressure is uncomfortable.

Can pressure be adjusted?

Yes. Tell the therapist what feels comfortable and speak up during the visit.

How is it different from Swedish massage?

Deep tissue is more focused and usually firmer; Swedish generally emphasizes flowing relaxation.

What session length should I choose?

The current options are 60, 90 and 120 minutes. Ask which fits your focus areas and preferences.
