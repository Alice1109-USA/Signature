Facial FAQs

Which facial should I choose?

Compare cleansing for buildup, hydration for dry-looking skin and anti-aging care for radiance and firmness-related appearance goals.

What happens during a facial?

Discuss skin goals and sensitivities first, then confirm which cleansing and care steps are included.

How often should I get a facial?

Frequency depends on the treatment and your skin. Ask for individual guidance rather than following a universal schedule.

Do you offer facials for dry or congested skin?

Yes. Explore hydration and deep cleansing, and discuss your skin before choosing a treatment.
