Massage Therapy Covered by Insurance

Our insurance-covered massage and medical massage services follow your healthcare provider's prescription. A licensed massage therapist tailors the session to the prescribed treatment needs, relevant instructions and your comfort.

We Accept Premera, Aetna and Kaiser

Signature Wellness SPA is an in-network massage therapy provider with Premera, Aetna and Kaiser in Redmond, WA. Your specific plan must still be checked: network participation alone does not guarantee massage therapy benefits, authorization or full payment.

How Insurance Verification Works

First-time insurance clients should complete the insurance benefits form and upload their insurance card information there before scheduling an insurance appointment. Then confirm benefits and any patient responsibility with us. Sending the form does not book an appointment or guarantee payment.

What to Send Before Your First Appointment

Use our insurance form to upload your insurance card information and provide the other details it requests. Have your prescription and any relevant referral or authorization information ready. For PIP or L&I visits, share your claim details and care instructions. Do not send sensitive information through the general website contact form.

Insurance Massage vs. Self-Pay Massage

Self-pay appointments follow the posted service menu. Insurance massage follows the prescription and applicable plan requirements; the amount a patient owes may differ from a listed service rate. Confirm deductibles, copays, coinsurance and any noncovered charges before treatment.

PIP & L&I Injury Massage

Car Accident Clients (PIP)
Bring your auto insurance claim details and correspondence, along with any medical referral from your provider.
Work Injury Clients (L&I)
Bring your L&I claim number and any documentation from your employer or physician regarding your work injury.
Personalized Treatment
Every session is customized to your needs by a licensed massage therapist familiar with post-accident and work-injury recovery.
What to Bring
Comfortable, loose clothing and notes about your pain areas and symptoms help us tailor your treatment from the first visit.

Insurance Massage FAQs

Does my insurance cover massage therapy?

Coverage depends on your plan and eligibility. New patients should request verification before treatment.

Do you accept Premera?

Yes. We are in-network with Premera, as well as Aetna and Kaiser, subject to the patient's specific plan and benefits.

Do I need a prescription or referral?

Our insurance massage is designed around the patient's healthcare provider's prescription. Confirm any additional referral or authorization requirements for your plan.

What information is needed for verification?

If this is your first time using insurance with us, complete the linked insurance benefits form and upload your insurance card information there. Have your prescription and relevant plan or claim documents available.

Are add-ons or tips covered?

Do not assume add-ons or gratuities are covered. Confirm noncovered charges and patient responsibility before your visit.

Can I use HSA/FSA?

Ask your plan administrator whether the service and required documentation meet your account's rules. We do not guarantee HSA/FSA eligibility.
