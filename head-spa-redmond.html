Personalized Scalp Care at a Slower Pace

Every appointment begins with a brief conversation about your scalp comfort, massage pressure and scent preferences. Warm water and mindful touch create space for deep relaxation, relief from everyday fatigue and a quieter pause from a demanding schedule.

  • Relaxation after screen time or a busy workweek
  • Comfort-focused care for desk-related neck and shoulder tension
  • Scalp cleansing and hair washing
  • Warm water and steam in selected rituals

Head SPA may help you unwind and feel more comfortable, but it is supportive self-care rather than medical treatment for pain, fatigue, or a scalp or hair condition. Tell us about sensitivities, recent procedures, allergies and product concerns before your appointment.

Head Spa FAQs

Which Head Spa should I choose?

Choose the 60-minute Signature wash for an essential reset, either 70-minute ritual for aromatherapy or herbal care, or the 90-minute Hydration Luxury Head Spa for the most complete experience.

Does every Head Spa include hair washing?

The Signature service centers on scalp cleansing and hair washing. Ask us to confirm the exact sequence and products included with your selected 70- or 90-minute ritual.

Which service includes steam and massage?

The 90-minute Hydration Luxury Head Spa includes warm steam, essential-oil head massage, an herbal hair soak, neck and shoulder massage and gentle lymphatic relaxation.

Is this a salon styling service?

A Head Spa booking does not automatically include salon styling or a blow-dry. Confirm any finishing services in advance.

What should I share before my appointment?

Tell us about scalp sensitivity, recent procedures, allergies, fragrance sensitivities and product concerns before your visit.

Is Head Spa a medical treatment?

No. Head Spa is a relaxation and self-care service and is not treatment for hair loss, dandruff or a medical scalp condition.
