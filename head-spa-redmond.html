Head Spa FAQs

What is a head spa?

A Head Spa is a head- and scalp-focused self-care appointment with gentle massage and time to relax.

Does a head spa include hair washing?

Yes. Our 90-minute Deep Scalp Cleansing Head Spa includes a hair wash and is priced at $125.

Can I add a hair mask, steam or peppermint oil?

These enhancements may be added to the Head Spa. Confirm availability, additional time and pricing when booking, and mention fragrance or essential-oil sensitivities.

How long does a session take?

Head Spa is available for 60 or 90 minutes. The 90-minute Deep Scalp Cleansing Head Spa with hair wash is $125. Confirm any extra time needed for add-ons.

Is this a salon styling service?

A Head Spa booking does not automatically include salon styling or a blow-dry. Confirm any finishing services in advance.

What should I do before my appointment?

Tell us about scalp sensitivity, recent procedures and product concerns. Ask whether you should arrive with clean or dry hair.
