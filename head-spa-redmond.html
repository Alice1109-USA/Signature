Head Spa FAQs

What is a head spa?

A Head Spa is a head- and scalp-focused self-care appointment. The specific steps depend on the option confirmed with our team.

Does a head spa include hair washing?

Ask whether washing is included in your selected service before booking; this page does not promise washing in every option.

How long does a session take?

Contact us to confirm the duration and price of the selected appointment.

Is this a salon styling service?

A Head Spa booking does not automatically include salon styling or a blow-dry. Confirm any finishing services in advance.

What should I do before my appointment?

Tell us about scalp sensitivity, recent procedures and product concerns. Ask whether you should arrive with clean or dry hair for the selected service.
