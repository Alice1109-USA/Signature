What Is Lomi Lomi Massage?

Lomi Lomi is a massage style associated with Hawaiian bodywork and known for flowing, rhythmic movements. This page describes the style without claiming a particular cultural lineage, certification or practitioner tradition.

The Lomi Lomi Experience

Long movements and an unhurried rhythm characterize this relaxation-focused option. Discuss pressure, areas of focus and personal boundaries before the appointment. The session should reflect your comfort, not an expectation to tolerate unwanted pressure.

How It Differs From Swedish Massage

Both Lomi Lomi and Swedish can offer a flowing massage experience. Lomi Lomi places particular emphasis on continuity and rhythm; Swedish is a familiar choice for guests seeking classic relaxation massage. Ask us which appointment fits your preferences.

What to Expect

Before booking, ask about the approach used for this service and share any positioning needs, sensitivities or areas to avoid. You can request an adjustment during the appointment. No medical or spiritual outcome is guaranteed.

Lomi Lomi FAQs

What does Lomi Lomi massage feel like?

It is typically described as flowing and rhythmic. Ask about the particular approach offered at your appointment.

Is Lomi Lomi deep pressure?

Not necessarily. Discuss your preferred pressure instead of assuming the service will be deep.

How is it different from Swedish massage?

Both can be flowing; Lomi Lomi places emphasis on rhythm and continuity, while Swedish is a classic relaxation style.

What should I expect at my first session?

Discuss pressure, positioning, boundaries and focus areas before beginning.
