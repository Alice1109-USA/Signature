Conditions We Treat

Back & Lower Back Pain

Back and lower back discomfort often results from poor posture, prolonged sitting, repetitive movements, stress, or overuse of muscles.

Home › Conditions We Treat › Back & Lower Back Pain

Benefits

Relieve Muscle Tension
Massage therapy targets the muscles in the back and lower back, helping to release tension caused by poor posture, prolonged sitting, or daily strain.
Reduce Stiffness & Soreness
Focused massage techniques ease stiffness and soreness in the upper and lower back, improving comfort and promoting natural relaxation.
Improve Mobility
By loosening tight muscles and improving circulation, massage therapy focuses on tight-feeling areas and movement-related comfort in the back and lower back.
Relax & Restore Balance
Regular massage relieves back discomfort while reducing stress, supporting overall relaxation and postural balance.
