Massage FAQs

What type of massage should I choose?

Compare relaxation-focused Swedish or Lomi Lomi with firmer deep tissue or targeted pain relief. Call if you are unsure.

How long should my massage session be?

The right length depends on the service and your goals. Check the pricing page and confirm availability.

Do you offer deep tissue massage?

Yes. Visit our deep tissue page for the approach and current session options.

Do you offer prenatal and postpartum massage?

Yes. Each has its own page, with comfort-focused information and guidance to discuss suitability before booking.

Can I use insurance for massage therapy?

We are in-network with Premera, Aetna and Kaiser. Verify your specific plan and prescription requirements first.

Do you offer couples massage?

Yes. Contact us to confirm arrangements and availability for two guests.
