Your First Massage Appointment: What to Expect and How to Prepare

Knowing what to wear, what to discuss and how a session works can make a first massage feel much more comfortable.

Short answer: Wear comfortable clothing, arrive with enough time to check in, and be ready to share your pressure preference, areas to focus on, areas to avoid and relevant health information. You remain in control of your comfort throughout the appointment.

Guests booking a first massage in Redmond often wonder what they should wear, whether they need to talk during the session and how much clothing they must remove. These are normal questions. A professional appointment should begin with clear communication and respect your privacy and comfort.

How early should I arrive?

Allow enough time to find Suite 4A, check in and avoid starting the appointment feeling rushed. If your booking confirmation gives a specific arrival time, follow that instruction. First visits may require more time for basic information and a pre-session conversation.

What should I wear to a massage?

Wear clean, comfortable clothing that is easy to change in and out of. Avoid complicated jewelry or accessories. What you wear during the massage depends on the service and your comfort level.

You should never feel pressured to remove more clothing than you want. Ask how draping works and discuss alternatives if you prefer to remain more covered. Only the area being worked on should be appropriately uncovered and draped for privacy.

What should I discuss before the session?

The most useful information is practical and specific. Explain how you want the appointment to feel rather than trying to select a technique based only on its name.

  • Your preferred pressure: light, medium or firm.
  • Areas you want prioritized, such as the neck, shoulders or back.
  • Any areas you do not want massaged.
  • Injuries, pregnancy, recent procedures, sensitivities or health factors relevant to comfort.
  • Whether you want quiet relaxation or occasional communication.

Do I have to talk during the massage?

No. You can enjoy a quiet appointment. However, speak up when something affects safety or comfort. Tell the therapist if pressure feels too strong, positioning is uncomfortable, you feel too warm or cold, or you want an area avoided.

Feedback is not an interruption. It is how the session stays personalized.

Should a massage hurt?

A massage does not need to hurt to feel worthwhile. Firmer or targeted work may feel intense, but sharp pain, numbness or pressure that makes you brace should be communicated immediately. The technique or pressure can be adjusted.

If you have new, severe or unexplained symptoms, massage should not replace appropriate medical evaluation.

Should I choose Swedish or deep tissue for my first visit?

Swedish massage is often chosen for a flowing, relaxation-focused experience. Deep tissue massage may fit guests who prefer slower, firmer work around specific areas of tightness. Read our complete Swedish vs. deep tissue comparison if you are undecided.

How long should my first massage be?

Thirty minutes is useful for a limited focus area. Sixty minutes suits many standard appointments. Ninety minutes or two hours provides more time for full-body massage plus targeted attention. Service availability and pricing differ, so review the current massage price menu.

Can I eat before a massage?

Avoid arriving immediately after a very heavy meal if that would make lying down uncomfortable. Normal hydration and a familiar routine are usually more practical than making major changes for the appointment.

What happens after the massage?

Take your time getting up and dressing. Individual responses vary: some guests feel relaxed, while others may notice temporary tenderness after focused pressure. Ask any service-specific questions before leaving and seek appropriate care if you experience concerning symptoms.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a different pressure?

Yes. Pressure can be adjusted before and during the session.

Can I ask not to have an area massaged?

Yes. Clearly identify any areas you want avoided.

What if I am late?

Contact the business as soon as possible. A late arrival may reduce available service time depending on the schedule.

Do I need to know exactly which massage to book?

No. Review the service descriptions, choose the closest match and explain your goals before the appointment.
