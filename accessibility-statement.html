Signature Wellness SPA is committed to ensuring this website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our Approach

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, including semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable menus, sufficient color contrast, and descriptive alt text for images.

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As we update this site, we review new pages and features for accessibility issues and address them promptly.

Feedback

If you encounter any barriers using this website, or need assistance accessing information about our services, please contact us — we're happy to help.
