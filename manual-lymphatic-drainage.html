Conditions We Treat

Manual Lymphatic Drainage

Gentle lymphatic massage supports daily lymphatic health or post-surgical recovery once the incision is healed and your doctor approves. We avoid treatment on open wounds, unhealed surgery sites, severe swelling, or infection — physician consultation is required before post-surgical massage.

Benefits

Gentle & Safe
Our massage uses light, careful techniques suitable for sensitive areas after surgery.
Ease Tension
Gentle touch reduces tension and promotes ease in affected areas.
Support Comfort
The massage encourages comfort and gentle relief for post-surgical or sensitive areas.
Relax Mind & Body
Light, soothing techniques help you feel calm and relaxed throughout your body.

Technique focuses on gentle, light touch along major lymph pathways, guiding lymph toward healthy lymph nodes in the neck, underarms, and groin — designed to be safe, soothing, and supportive for sensitive areas.
