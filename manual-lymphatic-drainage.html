What Is Manual Lymphatic Drainage?

Manual lymphatic drainage massage uses very light, rhythmic hand movements. It is different from deep tissue or vigorous full-body massage. We describe the service conservatively and do not claim that it detoxifies the body, boosts immunity, treats disease or guarantees a medical result.

What to Discuss Before Booking

Tell us about recent surgery, unexplained or severe swelling, infection, open wounds and any relevant medical restrictions. If you are seeking massage after a procedure or for a medical concern, obtain guidance from your treating healthcare provider before scheduling.

What to Expect During the Appointment

Your therapist will review comfort, positioning and areas to avoid. MLD is performed with gentle pressure at an unhurried pace. Ask for an adjustment or stop the session if anything feels uncomfortable.

Manual Lymphatic Drainage FAQs

Is manual lymphatic drainage a deep massage?

No. MLD uses light, rhythmic touch and should not be confused with firm-pressure deep tissue massage.

Can I book MLD after surgery?

Ask your treating healthcare provider when massage is appropriate. We do not work over open wounds or unhealed surgical sites, and medical clearance may be needed.

Does lymphatic massage detox the body?

We do not make detoxification, immunity or disease-treatment claims. This page describes a gentle massage service, not a medical cure.
