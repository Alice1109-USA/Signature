What Is Swedish Massage?

Swedish massage uses flowing strokes and adjustable, generally light-to-medium pressure. It is a relaxation-focused option for guests who want a calm session rather than consistently firm, targeted work.

What to Expect During Your Session

Before your massage, discuss pressure preferences, focus areas and any areas you would like to avoid. You can ask for less pressure or a change of position during the session; comfort is more important than enduring an intense sensation.

Who May Enjoy Swedish Massage?

This may suit guests seeking time to unwind, a first massage experience or a gentler approach to everyday muscle tension. Share health concerns or recent procedures before booking so we can discuss whether the appointment is appropriate.

Swedish vs. Deep Tissue Massage

Swedish massage generally emphasizes flowing, broad movements and relaxation. Deep tissue uses a more focused approach with firmer pressure where appropriate. Compare our deep tissue page if concentrated muscle tightness is your main concern.

Session Lengths & Pricing

Swedish massage is $60 for 30 minutes, $80 for 60 minutes, $120 for 90 minutes and $160 for 120 minutes. A longer appointment allows more time; it does not require deeper pressure.

Swedish Massage FAQs

Is Swedish massage gentle?

Usually it is a gentler option, but comfort and pressure preferences should be discussed before treatment.

What pressure is used?

Pressure is generally light to medium and can be adjusted during your appointment.

Swedish or deep tissue—which should I choose?

Choose Swedish for a flowing relaxation focus; compare deep tissue for more targeted, firmer work.

How long should I book?

The menu offers 60, 90 and 120 minutes. Consider how many areas you want to discuss and confirm with the team.
