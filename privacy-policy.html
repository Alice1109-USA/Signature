Signature Therapeutic Wellness ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through this website or by email, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any message content — including insurance card details you choose to send us for coverage verification.

Booking & Payments

Appointments and gift card purchases are processed through Square, a third-party payment and scheduling provider. Square's own privacy policy governs the information you provide during booking or checkout.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, schedule appointments, verify insurance coverage, and communicate with you about your care. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain client information for as long as necessary to provide services and meet recordkeeping obligations, including those required for insurance billing.
